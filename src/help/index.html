Made by Upkopp for copywriters, content strategists, and web designers who need to understand a website before changing its words.

Upkopp reads the page you choose and processes its content on your device. It has no account, analytics, or AI service. Website and image requests still contact their original hosts. Read the privacy policy.

Your first research pass

  1. Open a website and click the Upkopp toolbar icon. Content shows its title, description, headings, and paragraphs.
  2. Use Page map to understand the hierarchy and jump to a section. Expand relevant content on the website before collecting it.
  3. Choose Export JSON for Content and Page map in one file, or Capture text for a scrolling pass saved as plain text. Keep the source tab selected while capturing.
  4. Review the export, then bring the source material into your writing workflow. Upkopp does not write or rewrite the copy for you.

Choose the right export

  • Copy page text: the original text currently shown in Content, ready for your clipboard.
  • Export JSON: page text and metadata together with headings, landmarks, and sections. Optional image alt text is in Settings. No raw HTML or image bytes.
  • Capture text: a longer pass through the page. Completed captures save automatically by default; turn this off in Settings to review first.
  • Images → Download all images: one ZIP, with one folder of image files inside. Select all or choose individual images for a subset. Original hosts may require optional access.

Research context

Social tags show how a page describes itself for sharing. Indexability shows page-level canonical, robots, and sitemap signals. These support your content research; they are not a full SEO audit or proof of search-engine indexing.

If something is missing

Scroll or expand the website, then refresh Content or Images. Protected browser pages, closed components, embedded pages without access, and content drawn inside images may not be readable. Incomplete captures explain their limitations and wait for you to export them. A partial image ZIP includes a short failure report.

Capture results are temporary and clear when Chrome or the extension restarts. Downloaded files remain in your chosen folder. Use Forget session copies in Text capture to clear retained text results.

Make the workspace yours

Use Inspect to open the side panel or a larger window. Settings controls appearance, optional JSON alt text, automatic text saving, and save-location prompts. Poppins is bundled and works without a font-service connection.

For troubleshooting, include the Upkopp version, Chrome version, and a public example page in your support request. Do not share private client content. Public support details will be provided with the published listing.
